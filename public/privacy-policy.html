Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

At soldare.io, we deeply care about our users' privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled and how third-party services interact with our game.

1. Information We Collect

Our game does not directly collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. We do not intentionally target or collect data from children under the age of 13 (COPPA Compliance).

2. Web Analytics (Umami)

We use Umami, an open-source and privacy-focused web analytics tool, to analyze website traffic and improve user experience. Umami is entirely cookieless, does not collect any personal data, does not track users across multiple websites, and anonymizes all gathered technical information (such as page views, country, and browser type).

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA Compliance

We respect the privacy rights of users under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA). You can manage your cookie consent or opt-out of personalized tracking through your browser settings or the consent tools provided on our site.
